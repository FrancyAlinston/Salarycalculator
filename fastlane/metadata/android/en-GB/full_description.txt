**Salary Calculator** is a fast, modern, and privacy-focused Android application for calculating UK net salary, PAYE income tax, Class 1 National Insurance, pension deductions, overtime returns, statutory childcare subsidies, and umbrella payroll.

Built 100% with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose, Salary Calculator operates entirely offline on your device with zero ads, zero analytics, and zero tracking.

### Key Features 💡

- **HMRC PAYE Tax & NI Engine**:
  - Full support for 2024/2025 and 2025/2026 tax regimes.
  - Standard UK tax bands (Basic 20%, Higher 40%, Additional 45%).
  - Complete Scottish 6-Tier tax system (19%, 20%, 21%, 42%, 45%, 48%).
  - Class 1 Primary National Insurance with Primary Threshold and Upper Earnings Limits.
  - Dynamic tax code parsing (`1257L`, `BR`, `0T`, `D0`, `D1`, `NT`, `S...`, `C...`).
  - Personal Allowance reduction and 60% Marginal Tax Trap modeling above £100,000.

- **Childcare Subsidy & Entitlements Engine**:
  - 20% Tax-Free Childcare top-ups (up to £2,000/yr per child or £4,000 disabled).
  - 30 Hours / 15 Hours free childcare funding equivalents.
  - £100,000 Adjusted Net Income (ANI) cliff-edge detector with automatic pension sacrifice remedies.

- **Overtime & Shift Work Optimization**:
  - Overtime tax headroom and threshold monitor.
  - Interactive overtime multipliers (1.0x, 1.25x, 1.5x, 1.75x, 2.0x, 2.5x).
  - Rotational shift pattern generator (4-On 4-Off, Continental 2-2-3, Pitman, 3-Shift).
  - Multi-employer shift color coding and month-by-month heatmap.
  - UK payroll cutoff date rollover engine (Last Friday pay / Sunday cutoff).

- **Multi-Job & Inside-IR35 Umbrella Payroll**:
  - Dual-job cumulative tax aggregator modeling concurrent employers and independent NI thresholds.
  - Inside-IR35 Contractor Day Rate & Hourly Rate payroll with Employer NI (15.0% / 13.8%), Apprenticeship Levy (0.5%), and Employer Pension.

- **Privacy & Security**:
  - 100% Offline-first: No accounts, no servers, no ads, and no data tracking.
  - Biometric fingerprint/face lock for confidential payslip history.
  - 1-tap PDF payslip generation and RFC 5545 iCalendar (`.ics`) export.
