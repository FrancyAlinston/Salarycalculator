# Privacy Policy for Salary Calculator 🛡️

Last updated: September 5, 2026

**Salary Calculator** is built as an open-source, offline-first application. Your privacy and financial confidentiality are fundamental priorities.

---

### 1. Zero Data Collection
- **Salary Calculator does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal or financial information.**
- All salary numbers, hourly rates, tax codes, shift entries, and employer profiles entered into the application are stored **strictly on your local device** using encrypted Android Jetpack DataStore Preferences.

### 2. No Analytics & No Tracking
- The application contains **zero analytics SDKs, zero crash reporters, and zero tracking cookies**.
- We do not track how you use the application.

### 3. No Advertisements
- The application contains **zero third-party advertisements or ad networks**.

### 4. Device Permissions
- **Biometric Authentication (`USE_BIOMETRIC`)**: Used exclusively on-device to lock/unlock your private salary history cards and tax packs if you enable biometric protection in Settings.
- **Storage / File Sharing**: Used only when you explicitly export a PDF payslip, CSV ledger, or `.ics` shift calendar to your device storage or share via system share sheets.
- **Internet / Network**: If cloud backup (WebDAV/Nextcloud) or live HMRC rate synchronization is configured by you in Settings, network requests are made strictly directly to the server endpoints you define.

### 5. Open Source Transparency
The source code of Salary Calculator is publicly available and verifiable:
